# Design Direction

## Visual World

Accountant-system uses an operational editorial language: modern, slightly technological, precise and confident. The page should feel like a clear accounting workflow rather than a generic SaaS brochure.

## First Viewport

The header presents the `Accountant-system` wordmark, internal navigation, and the right-aligned `Ingresar` CTA. The hero leads with the real product sequence: attach a document, process it, validate the extracted information, save it when correct, and export it when ready. The right side visualizes that sequence as a document moving through named states.

## Composition

Use an asymmetric Swiss grid with generous whitespace, strong typographic scale, editorial labels and thin connector lines. The functionality section uses three centered cards on the soft blue supporting surface, with enough structure to feel like a deliberate system rather than a generic card grid. The hero workflow is the signature visual element and must remain understandable without animation.

## Palette

Preserve the existing warm off-white canvas, dark navy text, orange primary accent, soft blue supporting surfaces and blue secondary accent. Orange identifies the active action or current workflow state; blue identifies supporting information and stable states.

## Typography

Use a distinctive contemporary display face for the main headings and a restrained sans-serif for body copy. Small uppercase labels may use a technical tracking treatment for metadata and workflow states, but monospace should remain reserved for document values or system-like data.

## Interaction and Motion

The primary `Ingresar` CTA lives in the right side of the navbar and opens the configured accounting system in the current tab. Workflow states should be readable as static content first; any animation should be a single restrained progression from attached document to exportable state and must respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Responsive Behavior

On narrow screens, the hero stacks the copy before the workflow. The workflow becomes a vertical sequence with the document card above its states. The four process steps remain visible as a compact horizontal or wrapped sequence without horizontal scrolling.

## Boundaries

- Do not introduce screenshots, stock images, logos, fabricated metrics or unsupported customer claims.
- Keep the established orange and blue palette.
- Preserve the Spanish copy and the single primary CTA.
- Keep the user-visible mechanism grounded in attach, process, validate, save and export.
